# Local SEO Checklist (Cafe)

Use this when the user asks about **local SEO**, “rank on Google”, **Google Maps**, “near me” searches, or improving **discoverability**.

## Non-negotiables

- Use **real** business name/address/phone/hours from the codebase or provided by the user.
- Do not claim awards, ratings, “#1”, or certifications unless verified.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)

- **NAP consistency**: Name / Address / Phone match the website footer, contact page, and structured data exactly.
- **Primary category**: choose the closest official category (e.g., Cafe / Coffee shop) aligned with the actual offering.
- **Services / attributes**: pickup, delivery, dine-in (only what’s true).
- **Photos**: ensure a stable set (logo, storefront, interior, signature items) and a cadence for updates.
- **Posts**: weekly posts tied to real offers/events; avoid clickbait.
- **Q&A**: seed FAQs with accurate answers (halal, allergens, pickup/delivery, payment methods).

## Reviews (systematic, not spammy)

- **Moment to ask**: after a successful pickup/delivery confirmation or repeat purchase.
- **Channel**: short SMS/WhatsApp or email (based on the system’s existing messaging capabilities).
- **Prompt**: ask for a review + one specific detail (service speed, favorite drink) without incentivizing with heavy discounts.
- **Triage**:
  - Negative review: acknowledge, apologize where needed, offer a private resolution path.
  - Positive review: reply with short gratitude + local keyword naturally (area/city).

## On-site local SEO

- **Location page(s)** if multiple branches:
  - Address, map embed, hours, phone/WhatsApp, pickup instructions, parking notes.
  - Local keywords: city/area (only real served areas).
- **Internal linking**:
  - Home → location → menu → ordering CTA.
- **Technical**
  - Ensure indexable pages, correct canonical, no accidental `noindex`.
  - Fast LCP/CLS on mobile.

## Structured data (high impact)

- Add/verify JSON-LD for:
  - `LocalBusiness` / `CafeOrCoffeeShop`
  - `OpeningHoursSpecification`
  - `Menu` / `ItemList` where appropriate
- Ensure:
  - `name`, `address`, `telephone`, `url` are accurate and consistent.

## Keyword strategy (Egypt)

- Prefer local intent terms:
  - “coffee shop in [area]”, “cafe in [area]”, “specialty coffee [area]” (only if true)
- Build pages around:
  - locations, delivery/pickup, signature categories, FAQs (halal/allergens).
